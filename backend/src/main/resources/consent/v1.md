# Health & Wellness Consent (Version: v1)

Welcome to FitFlow Wellness. Before you share your health, fitness, and biometric data with us, please review this plain-language explanation of how your data is used, protected, and governed.

### 1. Purpose of Processing
Your health information is collected and processed exclusively for:
- Personalising fitness class suggestions and safety screening based on your physical condition and goals (`PERSONALIZED_CLASSES`).
- Calculating personal daily energy expenditure (BMR, TDEE) and class calorie burn estimates (`CALORIE_ESTIMATES`).
- Preparing anonymous, aggregated injury and modification summaries for trainers (`SHARE_TRAINER_SUMMARY`).
- Enabling AI coaching recommendations and conversation processing (`AI_ASSISTANT_PROCESSING`).

### 2. Information Collected
We collect the information you choose to enter into your fitness profile and wellness tracking:
- Experience level, past fitness history note, and fitness goals.
- Weekly session plans and general workout time availability.
- Reported injuries (area and severity) and health conditions.
- Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire (PAR-Q) screening responses (Q1–Q7).
- Self-reported medical clearance attestation.
- Biometrics including height, weight, sex, optional dietary preferences, and body metrics history.
- Post-session workout feedback and pain symptom reports.

### 3. Privacy, Access & Confidentiality
- **Member-Only Access**: Your decrypted health profile is visible only to you.
- **Trainers**: Trainers never see your personal health profile, names, conditions, PAR-Q answers, or free-text notes. When 3 or more members in a class have granted `SHARE_TRAINER_SUMMARY`, the instructor sees an anonymous aggregate count of injury areas (e.g. Knee: 2) to prepare safe movement modifications.
- **Administrators**: System administrators cannot access, view, or export your health data.
- **Third Parties & LLM Safety**: Your free-text notes, names, emails, exact age, height, weight, and PAR-Q responses are never sent to any large language model.

### 4. Storage & Security
All profile and biometric data is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Access is strictly audited in a tamper-resistant health access log.

### 5. Data Rights & Retention
You retain complete ownership of your data under applicable data protection laws:
- **View & Export**: You may view your profile at any time and export your full health records (`GET /api/v1/wellness/export`).
- **Withdraw Consent**: You can withdraw any consent purpose at any time (`DELETE /api/v1/wellness/consent/{purpose}`).
- **Hard Deletion**: You can permanently delete all health records, metrics, logs, and proposals at any time (`DELETE /api/v1/wellness/data`).

*Consent wording should be confirmed against your institution's requirements and India's DPDP Act obligations.*
